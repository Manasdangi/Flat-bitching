# Growth playbook: your part (do at your own pace)

## A. One-time setup (about 30 minutes total)

1. **Google Search Console** (search.google.com/search-console)
   - Add property → "URL prefix" → `https://www.kahanrahe.online`
   - Verify with the HTML tag method: copy the `<meta name="google-site-verification" ...>` tag and send it to Claude, who adds it to the site.
   - Sitemaps → submit `sitemap.xml`.
   - URL inspection → paste `/`, `/guides`, `/guides/best-sectors-gurgaon-single-women` → "Request indexing".
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools** (bing.com/webmasters): "Import from Google Search Console" is the fastest way.
3. **Domain** (optional but recommended): buy something like `gurgaonflatfinder.in`, then tell Claude. It gets connected in Vercel, and the sitemap and canonicals are updated. Do this before posting widely so links do not change later.
4. **Google Business-style presence** is not needed. Skip it.

## B. Posting (a few per week, as yourself)

Rules that keep you from being banned or ignored:
- Read each community's rules first. Post only where self-promotion is allowed, or comment inside a relevant thread.
- Lead with one useful fact, not the link. Put the link last, or only when asked.
- Reply to every comment for the first few hours.

## C. Who to ask directly
- 5–10 friends or colleagues in different sectors to answer polls (aim for 5+ votes in the sectors you know).
- Women-only PG owners, coliving brands and relocation groups: offer a free listing link back in exchange for a share.
- College alumni and company groups for people moving to Gurgaon.

## D. Check weekly
- Search Console → Performance: queries and pages getting impressions.
- Vercel Analytics: where visitors come from and which pages they open.
- Which community post drove visits: use a different link in each (e.g. `/?ref=reddit`), then read it in Vercel Analytics.
